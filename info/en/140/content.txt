Sort by progress: Sort by progress ascending, then by creation time descending.

Sort randomly: Shuffle flashcards.

Sort alphabetical by flashcard front: Sort alphabetical by flashcard front ascending, then alphabetical by flashcard back ascending. The order follows the rules of the language of the flashcard side if all flashcards in the list share it (e.g. ä: in German like a, in Swedish after z), otherwise those of the app language.

Sort by last repetition: Sort by time of last repetition descending, then by creation time descending.

Sort by mixed learning criteria: Sort by three criteria: low progress, progress / repetition counter ratio and randomly.
