You may create flashcards with a simple CSV file and import the core flashcards values to ReallyBasicVocab using the option in the app's menu.

The flashcards are stored in an automatically created filing box and deck and may be integrated into your existing filing boxes from there.

The file must be UTF-8 encoded (in Excel: “CSV UTF-8”); other files are rejected. Besides commas, the values may be separated by semicolons, tabs or vertical bars (|); the app chooses the separator that fits the first rows.
