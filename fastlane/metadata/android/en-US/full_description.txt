📣 Simple open-source app to manage your flashcards.

🔒 Your flashcards stay on your device: no account, no ads, no internet access.


🏛️ App structure: filing boxes -> decks -> flashcards


🗃️ Filing boxes can be exported as and imported from a backup (individually or all at once).

🗂️ The decks of a filing box contain the flashcards.

🌈 You may use four color variants for filing boxes and decks.


📇 Flashcards offer a front as well as a back side, optional notes and the learning progress.

🌐 You may set the language of the front and back for each filing box and / or deck.

🖌️ You may activate flashcard formatting and / or Markdown notes in the app settings.

📎 You may link images and other files to flashcards.

🏷️ You may add tags to flashcards.

🖨️ You may print flashcards.


▶️ From each flashcard list you may enter a query mode.

🔍 Flashcard lists for the entire collection as well as single filing boxes or decks are available. Using the “advanced query” option you may generate lists of all or some decks by tags, progress and / or last repetition.


📥 You may import a simple CSV file in the specified format (e.g. exported from a spreadsheet).


❗ Please back up your content on a regular basis.
